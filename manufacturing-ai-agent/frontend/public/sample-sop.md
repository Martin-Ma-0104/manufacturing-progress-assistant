## Cleaning evidence

The demonstration cleaning checklist requires recording the inspection date,
equipment identifier, approved procedure revision, and reviewer sign-off.
Cleaning completion must be verified against the site's approved SOP.

## Quality review

If inspection evidence is incomplete, request review by the authorized site team.
This sample does not specify chemicals, machine parameters or maintenance actions.

## Data interpretation

An abnormality event duration can overlap another event. Adding those durations
does not establish net machine downtime or a demonstrated root cause.
